W24 Monday · one brief, four visual worlds

Identical content — eyebrow, headline, subtitle from the real staged brief ("Your offer was accepted. The next 45 days are where deals fall apart.") — designed four radically different ways. The variable under test is design philosophy only. Her reaction to this set is the taste data the pipeline has never had.

01E-Editorial (current)

The approved standard: cream paper, Playfair serif, oxblood accent, contained photo plate. Restrained, tasteful, editorial.

If she picks this: the design was never the problem — look at topics, captions, or the faceless/static issue instead.
