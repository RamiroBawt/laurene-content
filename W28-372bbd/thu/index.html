How the earnest money deposit actually works — and when you get it back

Script · Educational explainer
Explainer ~75 sec on camera

Hook

Suggested cold-open — adopt or rework in your voice.

"People hear 'earnest money' and think it's a fee. It's not a fee.
Here's what it actually is, and when you get it back."

Beats

Structure + intent per beat. Deliver in your own words — these aren't lines to read verbatim.

  1. 0-8s Beat 1
    Open with the misconception: earnest money isn't a cost, it's a deposit that's still yours under the right conditions.
    Camera Tight headshot, looking direct.
  2. 8-25s Beat 2
    Define it plainly: earnest money is a good-faith deposit, usually 1-3% of the price, held in escrow — not paid to the seller, held by a neutral third party.
    Camera Same headshot.
    B-roll B-roll: a hand signing a document at a desk (3-4 sec).
  3. 25-55s Beat 3
    Walk through when it's refundable: if the deal falls apart during an active contingency (inspection, financing, appraisal) you filed on time, you get it back. If you back out for a reason not covered by a contingency, the seller can usually keep it.
    Camera Headshot, could gesture through the two scenarios.
  4. 55-75s Beat 4
    Close with the one thing to check before writing an offer: read your contingency deadlines and know them cold — that's what actually protects the deposit.
    Camera Direct eye contact, calm close.

Caption hook

Earnest money isn't a fee — it's yours under the right conditions. Here's when.
