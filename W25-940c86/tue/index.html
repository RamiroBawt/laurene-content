How offer competition works when it isn't about price

Script · Educational explainer
Explainer ~60 sec on camera

Hook

Suggested cold-open — adopt or rework in your voice.

"You offered the full asking price. You got rejected. Here's what actually happened."

Beats

Structure + intent per beat. Deliver in your own words — these aren't lines to read verbatim.

  1. 0-6s Beat 1
    Cold open with the scenario. No preamble — the hook is the frustration.
    Camera Tight headshot, direct.
  2. 6-20s Beat 2
    In a market with limited supply, sellers often receive multiple offers simultaneously. A full-price cash offer loses to a full-price offer with fewer contingencies. A full-price offer with a 45-day close loses to one that closes in 21. Price is one variable. It's often not the deciding one.
    Camera Headshot. Conversational — she's walked buyers through this dozens of times.
  3. 20-40s Beat 3
    The three things sellers in a tight supply market look at beyond price: (1) cash vs. financed — cash removes the appraisal risk; (2) contingency count — inspection, financing, appraisal each add seller uncertainty; (3) close timeline — faster close reduces carrying cost for the seller. None of these require you to waive protections. They require you to understand what you're competing against.
    Camera Count with fingers if natural. One clear statement per point.
    B-roll Optional: a simple document on desk — three bullet points visible but illegible. 2-3 seconds.
  4. 40-55s Beat 4
    What a buyer can do: ask what terms matter most to this specific seller before writing. A seller who needs a 60-day close because they're building elsewhere is not the same as a seller who wants out fast. The conversation with your agent before the offer is where you find this out.
    Camera Headshot. Practical, calm.
  5. 55-60s Beat 5
    Close: 'Losing a full-price offer is frustrating. Understanding why makes the next one more competitive.'
    Camera Direct, warm. Clean end.

Caption hook

Full asking price isn't always enough in a market with three offers on the same home.
