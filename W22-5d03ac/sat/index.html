Fort Lauderdale isn't Miami — and that's not a consolation prize

Script · Hot take
Hot-take ~45 sec on camera

Hook

Suggested cold-open — adopt or rework in your voice.

"Everyone calls Fort Lauderdale the cheaper Miami. I'd push back on that framing.
They're two different cities making two different bets on what coastal Florida looks like."

Beats

Structure + intent per beat. Deliver in your own words — these aren't lines to read verbatim.

  1. 0-5s Beat 1
    Open with the take, stated plainly: FTL is not 'Miami but cheaper.' It's a different city with a different identity — mid-century residential, Intracoastal-based, walkable at a different scale.
    Camera Tight headshot, direct. No soft lead-in.
  2. 5-20s Beat 2
    The case: FTL's differentiators aren't about price — they're structural. The Intracoastal access is direct. The residential neighborhoods (Coral Ridge, Victoria Park, Rio Vista) are mid-century, owner-occupied, walkable to real restaurants and the beach. The neighborhoods that made Miami famous are now overbuilt or priced beyond the segment Laurène works with. The FTL equivalent has more character and more negotiating room.
    Camera Headshot. Could hold a neighborhood map as b-roll cut.
    B-roll 3-sec aerial Coral Ridge canal-and-homes shot — mid-century rooftops, water, palms. Then back to headshot.
  3. 20-35s Beat 3
    Acknowledge the counter: Miami has Brickell, Wynwood, the design district. The energy is real. If that's what you're looking for — go. But buyers who came to FTL because it was 'cheaper Miami' and stayed because the lifestyle was actually what they wanted — those are the ones who got it right. The framing reversed for them.
    Camera Headshot. Conversational, not defensive.
  4. 35-45s Beat 4
    Close: 'If you're comparing cities, I'd flip the framing. Don't ask what FTL has that Miami doesn't. Ask what you actually want — and then see which city was built for that.'
    Camera Direct. Warm. End clean.

Caption hook

Everyone calls Fort Lauderdale 'cheaper Miami.' I'd reframe that entirely.
